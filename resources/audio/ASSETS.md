# Original audio assets

All eleven files are generated by `tools/GenerateAudio.java`, using original note
sequences and mathematical oscillators. No downloaded recordings, third-party
samples, external libraries or additional attribution requirements are involved.
The generator is included so these project assets can be reproduced and edited.

Format: RIFF WAV, signed little-endian PCM, 22050 Hz, 16 bit, mono.

| File | Duration | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |

| lobby_bgm.wav | 18.462 s | "Harbor of Maps", eight 4/4 bars in D major at 104 BPM; quieter melody, plucks and chords |

The music wraps note tails and delayed melody into the beginning of the same
buffer, preserving continuity across Clip.LOOP_CONTINUOUSLY repetitions.
All assets are included directly in the client JAR at `/audio/<name>.wav`.
This manifest and the generator are not required on a deployed client machine.
